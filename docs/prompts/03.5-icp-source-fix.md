### TechCrunch URL adjustment

Mentor specified `https://techcrunch.com/category/asia/feed/`. That URL returns 404 — TechCrunch migrated their CMS in late 2024 and category-based feed URLs no longer exist. The equivalent working URL is `https://techcrunch.com/tag/asia/feed/` (tag-based). Same intent, same content, working URL. Verified by direct curl before applying.

## Why these specific replacements (and the uncertainty)

The mentor's directive said "replace with new India source" / "replace with new SEA source" without naming specific URLs. The Prompt 3.5 spec containing those URLs wasn't pasted in. So I picked:

- **StartupTalky** (India) — established Indian startup news site, standard WordPress `/feed/`, less likely to bot-block than VC outlets. Confirmed working in smoke test (2 leads on first run).
- **Vulcan Post** (SEA) — Singapore-based tech publication, smaller than TechInAsia and less aggressive about bot blocking. Feed loads cleanly; produces 0 leads on quiet news days because their RSS isn't funding-announcement-heavy.

**Also tried but discarded:** DealStreetAsia (returned 503, likely Cloudflare blocking same as TechInAsia).

## Why YC `maxPages` 10 → 20 didn't add leads

YC has roughly 50 pages total (1000 companies). With `isHiring=true`, pages 1-20 = first 400 companies. India/SEA companies are ~0.5% of YC's portfolio. Going from 200 to 400 sampled doesn't statistically move the needle from "2 leads." To significantly increase YC pull from our geos we'd need to:

- Page through the entire 50-page list (politeness concerns), OR
- Use YC's filter API more precisely (currently the `batch` filter appears server-side-broken), OR
- Accept that YC is a small percentage of our pipeline and lean on RSS.

The 20-page setting is harmless either way — the scraper's loop short-circuits when `nextPage` is null, so on quiet days it stops naturally.
